TrainOS AI — Fitness Superapp

A fitness application concept combining workout recommendations, nutrition tracking, and machine learning to explore a more personalised approach to fitness.

01 / OVERVIEW

Bringing fitness tracking and recommendations together.

The concept

TrainOS AI was designed around the idea of bringing fitness-related features into one mobile application. The concept combined workout recommendations with nutrition tracking to explore how users could manage different aspects of their fitness journey in one place.

The implementation

The application used React Native and Expo for the mobile experience, with a Flask backend supporting application functionality. A TensorFlow/Keras model explored classifying fitness recommendations based on inputs such as calorie intake and workout type.

04 / MACHINE LEARNING

Exploring personalised workout classification.

The machine-learning component explored whether fitness-related inputs could be used to classify workout recommendations into different categories.

Model approach

A TensorFlow/Keras model was developed to explore three output categories: Strength, Endurance, and Flexible. Calorie intake and workout type were among the intended input features.

The model was part of an exploratory prototype, rather than a validated system for determining an individual's optimal workout.

Evaluation and limitations

The model achieved approximately 34% accuracy during development. This indicated that its predictions were not yet reliable enough to support confident fitness recommendations.

The limited dataset, including synthetic data, was an important constraint. Further work would require better representative data, model evaluation, and iterative experimentation.

Reported accuracy is an approximate development result, not a benchmark of a production-ready model.
